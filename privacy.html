Omingle is an adults-only platform strictly for users aged 18 and over. By accessing Omingle, you represent and warrant that you are at least 18 years of age, or the age of majority in your jurisdiction, whichever is greater.

We take the safety of minors very seriously. If you believe you have encountered a minor on our platform, you must take the following steps immediately:

  • Report the user — use the in-session report button to flag the individual.

Section 03

Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be transparent about what we do not do with your conversations. Your live sessions are private by default:

  • No video or audio recordings: We do not record, store, or archive your video or audio streams at any point. Once your session ends, no media from that conversation is retained on our servers.
  • No biometric data: We do not collect or process any biometric information such as facial geometry, voice prints, or any other biometric identifiers from your live stream.
  • No real-time monitoring: Our staff do not actively watch or listen to your live sessions. Automated content moderation tools may analyze behavioral signals (e.g., report frequency) but do not record or store your media.
  • No location beyond IP: We do not use GPS or precise geolocation tracking. The only location inference we may make is a general regional estimate derived from your IP address.
  • No government-issued IDs: We do not collect identity documents, social security numbers, passport details, or similar sensitive government identifiers.
