Omingle – Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Omingle uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, ensure platform functionality, and protect user safety. By continuing to use Omingle, you agree to our use of cookies as described below.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, improve performance, and maintain essential functionality. Cookies used by Omingle do not personally identify you.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Omingle uses minimal cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core features such as security, fraud prevention, and page loading.
  • Preference Cookies: Store user choices such as theme settings or language preferences.
  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that help manage your connection during active use.
  • Safety & Moderation Cookies: Used to detect abuse, enforce bans, and prevent repeated violations.

3. Cookies We Do NOT Use

For transparency, Omingle does not use:

  • User tracking cookies
  • Behavioral advertising cookies
  • Third‑party marketing cookies
  • Cross‑site tracking or profiling technologies

4. Third‑Party Cookies

Some third‑party services may place their own cookies, such as:

  • Hosting or CDN providers (for performance)
  • Security and anti‑abuse systems
  • Google Ads (if enabled on your site)

These cookies follow the third party’s own privacy policies. Omingle does not control these cookies directly.

5. How You Can Control Cookies

Most browsers allow you to:

  • Block or delete cookies
  • Disable third‑party cookies
  • Clear browsing data
  • Enable “Do Not Track” (DNT) mode

Note: Disabling essential cookies may affect Omingle’s functionality, including video matching and connection stability.

6. Policy Updates

Omingle may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Updates will take effect immediately upon posting. Continued use of the service indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

By using Omingle, you acknowledge and accept the use of cookies as described in this policy.
