Community Guidelines

Welcome to Omingle — a random chat platform for adults to connect with new people worldwide. To keep the community respectful and safe, everyone must follow these guidelines.

Violations may result in warnings, temporary or permanent bans, IP/device restrictions, or reports to authorities when required.

1. Purpose of These Guidelines

  • Define acceptable behavior
  • Prevent harmful interactions
  • Explain enforcement actions
  • Protect user safety

2. Age Requirement

  • 18+ only
  • No misrepresentation of age
  • No minors on camera or nearby
  • Report suspected minors immediately

3. Respectful Conduct

  • Be polite and courteous
  • Disconnect if uncomfortable
  • Respect cultural differences

4. Unacceptable Behavior

4.1 Harassment & Bullying

  • Insults or mocking
  • Threats or aggressive language
  • Unwanted attention

4.2 Hate Speech

  • Racial or discriminatory slurs
  • Extremist content
  • Targeting protected groups

4.3 Illegal or Dangerous Behavior

  • Violence or threats
  • Self‑harm encouragement
  • Illegal activity discussions

4.4 Scams & Fraud

  • Requesting money
  • Phishing attempts
  • Identity theft

4.5 Impersonation

  • Pretending to be others
  • Pretending to be Omingle staff

5. Privacy Rules

  • No sharing personal details
  • No recording or screenshots
  • No reposting conversations

6. Enforcement

  • Warnings
  • Temporary bans
  • Permanent bans
  • IP/device blocking

7. Final Agreement

By using Omingle, you agree to follow and respect these Community Guidelines.
