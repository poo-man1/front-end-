Community Guidelines

Welcome to Omingle — a random chat platform for adults to connect with new people worldwide. To keep the environment respectful and safe, everyone must follow these Community Guidelines.

Violations may result in warnings, temporary bans, permanent bans, IP/device restrictions, or reporting to authorities when required.

1. Purpose of These Guidelines

These rules explain:

  • Acceptable behavior
  • Unacceptable behavior
  • How Omingle enforces rules
  • How to protect yourself
  • How to report harmful interactions

2. Age Requirement

  • Users must be 18+.
  • No misrepresenting age.
  • No minors allowed on camera or nearby.
  • Report suspected minors immediately.

3. Respectful Conduct

  • Use polite language.
  • Maintain a friendly tone.
  • Disconnect if uncomfortable.
  • Respect cultural and language differences.

4. Unacceptable Behavior

4.1 Harassment & Bullying

  • Insults or name‑calling
  • Mocking or ridiculing
  • Unwanted attention
  • Aggressive or threatening language

4.2 Hate Speech & Discrimination

  • Slurs targeting identity or appearance
  • Promoting extremist ideologies
  • Spreading hate against protected groups

4.3 Illegal or Dangerous Behavior

  • Violence or threats
  • Displaying weapons dangerously
  • Encouraging self‑harm
  • Discussing illegal activities
  • Selling prohibited items

4.4 Scams & Fraud

  • Requesting money
  • Scam attempts
  • Requesting financial information
  • Phishing or identity theft

4.5 Impersonation

  • Pretending to be another person
  • Pretending to be a celebrity
  • Pretending to be Omingle staff

5. Privacy Expectations

5.1 Do Not Share Personal Information

  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Email
  • Financial info
  • Social media accounts
  • Private photos or documents

5.2 Do Not Request Personal Information

  • Do not pressure others for sensitive details.

5.3 No Recording Policy

  • No video recording
  • No audio recording
  • No screenshots
  • No screen‑recording software
  • No reposting conversations

6. Guidelines for Video & Audio

6.1 Be Presentable

  • Be fully clothed
  • No disturbing gestures
  • No graphic content

6.2 Keep the Environment Safe

  • No weapons
  • No drugs or paraphernalia
  • No disturbing posters
  • No people in the background without consent

6.3 Avoid Misleading Appearances

  • No deepfakes
  • No deceptive filters
  • No prerecorded loops

7. Respect Boundaries

  • Stop when asked
  • Do not force conversations
  • Disconnect if uncomfortable

8. Random Matching Rules

  • Matches are unpredictable
  • You cannot choose who appears
  • No blaming users for appearance or location

9. Reporting Violations

  • Report real violations
  • Provide accurate information
  • Do not misuse reporting

10. Enforcement Actions

  • Warnings
  • Temporary bans
  • Permanent bans
  • IP/device restrictions
  • Reporting to authorities

11. Tips for Staying Safe

  • Do not share personal info
  • End uncomfortable chats
  • Report violations
  • Keep your device secure

12. Healthy Communication Practices

  • Listen actively
  • Avoid interrupting
  • Respect cultural differences

13. Content Restrictions

  • No graphic violence
  • No harmful organizations
  • No unsafe activities
  • No disturbing imagery

14. Platform Integrity

  • No hacking
  • No bots
  • No exploits
  • No malicious code

15. User Responsibility

  • Be accountable
  • Be respectful
  • Follow community standards

16. Cultural Sensitivity

  • Do not mock accents
  • Do not insult nationalities
  • Respect differences
  • Be patient

17. Third‑Party Links

  • Do not share external links
  • Do not share downloads
  • Do not share suspicious websites

18. Compliance with Laws

Users must follow all applicable laws and regulations.

19. Updates to Guidelines

Guidelines may be updated periodically. Continued use means acceptance.

20. Final Agreement

By using Omingle, you agree to follow all Community Guidelines.
