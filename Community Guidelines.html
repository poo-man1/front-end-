Omingle – Community Guidelines

Omingle is a platform designed for friendly, respectful, and spontaneous conversations with people from around the world. Our goal is to maintain a safe environment where users feel comfortable communicating openly. These Community Guidelines provide clear expectations for proper conduct and behavior. By using Omingle, you agree to follow these guidelines at all times.

1. Be Respectful

Every user deserves a positive and respectful experience. Treat others how you would want to be treated. Avoid using language or actions that may hurt, offend, or threaten someone. Respect differing viewpoints, backgrounds, identities, and cultures.

2. No Hate or Discrimination

You may not use Omingle to engage in hateful or discriminatory speech. This includes insults or hostility targeted at individuals or groups based on:

  • Race or ethnicity
  • Religion
  • Nationality
  • Gender identity or expression
  • Sexual orientation
  • Disability
  • Appearance or body type

3. No Harassment or Bullying

Harassment creates an unsafe environment and is not tolerated. Examples include:

  • Threats or intimidation
  • Repeated unwanted messages
  • Name‑calling or verbal abuse
  • Mocking accents, traits, or disabilities
  • Encouraging harmful behavior

4. No Illegal or Unsafe Activities

You may not use Omingle for anything illegal or harmful, including:

  • Drug-related activity
  • Violence or threats
  • Scams, fraud, or impersonation
  • Unauthorized financial requests

5. Absolutely No Nudity or Sexual Content

Omingle strictly prohibits nudity, sexual behavior, or sexually suggestive acts. This rule protects everyone and keeps Omingle safe and friendly.

6. No Recording or Screenshotting

Recording or saving any part of a conversation—including audio, video, or text—is prohibited. This protects user privacy and prevents misuse of content.

7. Protect Your Personal Information

Do not share or request:

  • Phone numbers
  • Addresses
  • Emails
  • Bank details or passwords
  • Private social media accounts

Strangers online should never receive sensitive information from you.

8. Use the Report Feature Responsibly

The report button exists to maintain safety. Report users who violate these rules. Do not abuse the report function to harm innocent users.

9. Disconnection Etiquette

Users may disconnect at any time. Do not guilt‑trip, insult, or pressure someone for leaving a chat. Respect boundaries and personal comfort levels.

10. Respect Local Laws

Users from around the world join Omingle. You are responsible for knowing and following the laws of your region while using the platform.

11. No Bots or Automation

Using scripts, bots, or automated tools to access Omingle is against the rules and may lead to permanent bans.

12. Responsible Content Behavior

You may not show disturbing, violent, or graphic content. This includes imagery or behavior meant to shock or upset other users.

13. Safety First

Your safety is our priority. Always trust your instincts and use the disconnect or report buttons when uncomfortable. Do not feel obligated to continue any conversation.

14. Repeated Violations

Users who repeatedly violate guidelines may receive temporary or permanent bans. These bans may extend across devices, networks, or IP addresses.

Thank you for making Omingle a safe, fun, and positive environment for everyone.
