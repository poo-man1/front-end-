Omingle – Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Omingle (“we,” “our,” or “the platform”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you access or use our random video chat and messaging services. By using Omingle, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully to understand how your data is handled. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, you must discontinue using the platform immediately.

1. Introduction

Omingle provides an anonymous, real‑time communication service that connects users with strangers for video, audio, or text chat. As an entertainment-based platform, we prioritize user privacy, safety, and anonymity. We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to operate the platform effectively and securely. Our goal is to ensure that your interactions remain private, safe, and unrecorded.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Omingle does not collect or store:

  • Video or audio recordings
  • Message content from chats
  • Usernames or account data (since accounts are not required)
  • Photos, files, contacts, or device media
  • Biometric identifiers

All video and audio streams occur through real‑time peer‑to‑peer (WebRTC) technology whenever possible. This means the content of your calls does not pass through or get stored on our servers.

3. Information We Collect Automatically

To provide a secure and stable service, Omingle may collect limited technical information, including:

  • IP address (used for matching, security, and abuse prevention)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type, operating system, and network information
  • Connection timestamps
  • Approximate geographic region (city/state level only)
  • Session IDs (temporary and auto‑deleted)

This information is necessary for maintaining platform performance, preventing fraud, blocking abusive users, and ensuring compatibility.

4. Personal Information You Provide

Since Omingle does not use accounts or registrations, you are not required to submit personal data such as:

  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Profile information
  • Passwords

However, if you voluntarily choose to contact us through email for support, we may receive and store your email and message content solely for communication purposes.

5. How We Use the Information We Collect

Data we collect is used only to:

  • Enable matching between users
  • Ensure platform stability and performance
  • Prevent fraud, spam, and misuse
  • Investigate reports about abusive behavior
  • Protect the safety and wellbeing of users
  • Improve our systems and fight malicious activity

We NEVER use your data for:

  • Advertising personalization
  • User profiling
  • Tracking across websites

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Omingle uses minimal cookies and local storage items, including:

  • Session tokens (temporary)
  • Preference cookies for dark mode or language settings
  • Security cookies to prevent abuse

These cookies do not track you across other websites and are used solely to enhance your user experience.

7. Third‑Party Services

Omingle may work with third-party services that help maintain or analyze platform functionality, such as:

  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics tools (anonymous metrics only)
  • Content delivery networks (CDNs)

We ensure that any third-party service we use adheres to strict privacy and security standards. These providers do not have access to your video, audio, or chat contents.

8. Reporting & Moderation

When you report another user, we may collect:

  • The reported user’s IP address
  • Timestamp of the incident
  • A general description of the violation

We may temporarily log metadata related to the session to investigate the issue and, if necessary, apply warnings or bans.

9. Data Retention

We retain only the minimum information needed to operate securely. For example:

  • Temporary session logs: deleted automatically within hours
  • Ban records: stored only for enforcement
  • Support emails: stored until resolved

We do not store data longer than necessary.

10. Data Sharing

Omingle does NOT share your data with:

  • Advertisers
  • Data brokers
  • Third-party marketing companies

We may only disclose limited information if required by:

  • Court order
  • Legal request
  • To respond to credible threats or prevent harm

11. Security Measures

We take strong precautions to protect user data, including:

  • Industry‑standard encryption
  • Secure servers and firewalls
  • Regular software updates
  • Strict access controls

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under data protection laws, including rights to:

  • Access your data
  • Request deletion
  • Limit processing
  • Withdraw consent

Because Omingle stores minimal data, these requests are usually simple to process.

13. Children's Privacy

Omingle is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If a minor is discovered using the platform, we will remove access.

14. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Your continued use of Omingle after updates signifies your acceptance of the revised policy.

Thank you for trusting Omingle. Your privacy and safety are our top priorities.
